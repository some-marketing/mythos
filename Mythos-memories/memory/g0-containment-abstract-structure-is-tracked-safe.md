Operator ruled 2026-08-02: abstract structural simulation doctrine (scale tiers, carriage/authority splits, tick/turn vocabulary, topology findings) MAY live at tracked repo paths during the local-only phase. World-specific content (lore, identities, canon, protected-corpus-derived material) stays untracked. Decision record: `_dev/concepts/simulation-worldbuilding-doctrine-port/operator-decision-20260802-g0-containment.md`.

**Why:** the distinction that matters is structure vs content, not tracked vs untracked — and absorbed abstractions count as content ([[proper-noun-leak-scan-is-not-a-leak-scan]]).

**How to apply:** don't re-raise the tracked-vs-untracked question for purely structural sim artifacts; do keep destination reviews for every move ([[crow-world-local-only-superseded-with-sequencing]]) and source-check abstractions before tracking them.
