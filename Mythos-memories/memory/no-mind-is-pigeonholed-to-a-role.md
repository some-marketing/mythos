Operator ruling 2026-08-04: "codex is to be freed up to fill all roles. we need to
assign the right minds to the right jobs not have any pigeonholed." No registered mind
is bound to a fixed role. Codex may code, review, read, or synthesize; the same holds
for gemini, Claude tiers, and codewhale. This supersedes the pigeonholing aspect of
earlier role divisions (e.g. the 2026-07-03 "coder=Claude workers, codex→review lane"
correction recorded in /dlx) — those are now default hints, not bindings.

**Why:** Fixed role-to-mind bindings waste the best-fit mind for a given job and
bottleneck lanes on one harness. Fit-per-dispatch is the actual routing question:
cheapest/best mind this lane's verification can hold accountable.

**How to apply:** At every dispatch, choose the mind by job fit and disclose it.
Strengths are hints (codex strong on code, gemini on search/long-context reads).
Addendum 2026-08-04: "we touch all minds and use all harnesses to better ourselves" —
hermes and pi are dispatchable too (see dispatch-routing-rule
additional_dispatchable_harnesses); a session that routes every lane to the same two
harnesses out of habit is leaving registered intelligence idle.
Invariants that still bind: reviewer must be distinct-family from the producer
(producer-never-validates-own-trial); codewhale is PRC-hosted → non-sensitive only;
PII/credential payloads → opencode-local ([[local-models-are-pii-lane-only]]); codex
dispatches still go through the managed bridge
([[codex-dispatch-must-use-managed-bridge]]).
