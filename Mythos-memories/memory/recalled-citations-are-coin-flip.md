2026-08-02, Q-B citation audit: a research leg honestly flagged which of its 20 findings had unverified bibliographic details. Of the unverified set that was spot-checked, one in two was wrong (MAX-MIN Ant System venue issue number) while the *substance* of the same findings was good — the leg's best catch (the Eccles listening-gate) came from its unverified set.

**Why:** recall reproduces claims far more reliably than identifiers; arXiv IDs, DOIs, volume/issue numbers are exactly the details that look authoritative and verify worst.

**How to apply:** any citation heading into a committed/ratifiable artifact gets its identifier live-verified; where sources disagree on a field, drop the field rather than assert either value; record the provenance (verified vs recalled) in the artifact itself. A research leg that flags its own unverified set is doing it right — the flag is load-bearing, reward it. Related: [[corrections-do-not-propagate-themselves]].
