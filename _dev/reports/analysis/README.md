# `reports/analysis/`

The hot surface for anything a session produces while thinking something through:
review findings, debrief writeups, audit results, quest-charter drafts. Plain gloss:
your analysis/scratch-report directory — one file per finding or decision, human- and
machine-readable.

Files here follow the lifecycle in `../../policies/data-handling.md`: active while the
work is live, finished when the producing workflow closes, then eligible for archive
under `../../archive/`.

Subdirectories: `task-plans/` (quest charters awaiting or under trial), `convene-runs/`
(records of multi-mind council deliberation).
