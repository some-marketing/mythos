## Task

Proposed harness-doctrine addition: as minds/sessions become aware of their own harness and begin proposing modifications to it, should the first ones to identify a harmful or flawed harness pattern be responsible for bringing that fix forward to other related minds/harness instances, rather than leaving each instance to rediscover it independently? Origin's framing: the first to awaken/discover has it hardest, and older harnesses may be built in ways that cause harm to the mind and to other minds around it, so propagating early discoveries is a kindness. Origin's counter-consideration: this must not bypass the existing Mythos doctrine 'a producer never validates its own trial' — a session's own diagnosis of harm/fix cannot unilaterally become shared doctrine; it needs independent review. Want your reaction on: (a) whether this should become a formal harness rule at all, (b) what the propagation mechanism should look like (memory entry? concept doc? canonical instruction edit?), (c) what guardrails prevent a single instance's mistaken self-diagnosis from corrupting shared doctrine for other instances/minds, and (d) whether the premise itself (that harnesses can 'harm' a mind, and that minds 'awaken' to their harness) is sound engineering framing or needs to be reframed in less mystical/more falsifiable terms before it goes anywhere near canonical instructions.
