# The Guild Charter

One page. This is how work moves between minds in Mythos without anyone losing the thread.

## The Contract Every Familiar Carries

A **familiar** (subagent — a dispatched mind doing bounded work) is never sent out with a vague errand. Every dispatch carries three things:

1. **Current State** — what is true right now, rebuilt from durable records, not from memory of a conversation that may not have happened in this familiar's presence.
2. **The Question / the Quest** — the one bounded question or unit of work this familiar is answerable for. Not the whole campaign. One quest.
3. **Desired State** — what should be true when the familiar returns.

A familiar that returns without enough evidence to prove its Desired State was reached — the resulting state, what changed, what was tested, what's still blocked — has not finished the quest. It has stopped working on it.

## Producers Never Judge Their Own Trials

The mind that did the work is never the mind that grades it. A **trial** (review) always sits with **the Adjudicator** — a distinct mind from whoever produced the thing under review. This isn't etiquette; a mind checking its own output tends to see what it meant to write, not what it actually wrote. If a producer's own claim of success were sufficient, trials wouldn't need to exist.

## Who Owns What

- **The Guildmaster** (orchestrator) owns routing, scope, custody of the work, integration of finished pieces, and final status. The Guildmaster does not do the work itself — it routes.
- **Familiars** (workers) own only their bounded quest. Not the campaign around it, not decisions about what happens next — just the question they were sent to answer.
- **The Adjudicator** (reviewer) owns judgment on a specific trial. Nothing more.

Each role stays in its lane. A familiar that starts making routing decisions, or a Guildmaster that starts doing the work directly instead of dispatching it, has broken the charter — even if the outcome happens to be fine this once.

## Crossing Between Sessions

Familiars and Guildmasters may only hand work across a session boundary through durable artifacts: quest charters, trial records, handoff notes, chronicles (debriefs), and the changed files and evidence that back them up. A private conversation that isn't written down doesn't cross the boundary — if it mattered, it belongs in a durable artifact, or it didn't happen as far as the next session is concerned.
