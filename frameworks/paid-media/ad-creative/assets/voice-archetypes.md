# Voice Archetypes (fallback when no client exemplar exists)

Per the `ad-creative-human-voice` convene: a real client exemplar is always preferred. When none exists, the framework must NOT fall back to bare adjectives — it falls back to one of these **archetypes**, each anchored by real human-voiced samples, and warns loudly that a default voice is in use. Replace with a real exemplar as soon as one is available.

> Usage rule: pick ONE archetype, name the speaker, then write *as that speaker would say it to this buyer in this moment*. These samples set cadence and register — not content.

## 1. Plain-Spoken Local Owner ("the Neighbour")
Best for local/community businesses (dealers, clinics, trades, shops). Direct, warm, price-honest, no gloss. Real asks.
- ✅ "Come take one for a drive. We're right here in town — no run to the city."
- ✅ "$310 every two weeks, all-wheel drive standard. That's the whole story."
- ✅ "Bought a car from us? This summer your golf's on us."

## 2. Straight-Shooter Operator (the operator's own register — see [[feedback_client-email-voice-casual-direct]])
Lead with the ask, cut corporate justification, short sentences, specifics.
- ✅ "New CX-5, $310 bi-weekly. Offer's up June 30. Come see us."
- ✅ "Free oil changes for a year with any new truck. That's it. That's the deal."

## 3. Warm Storyteller (sparingly)
For brand/emotional moments, not payment ads. One small true scene, then the point.
- ✅ "First nice Saturday of the year, the lot fills up with people just kicking tires. Come kick a few."

## ❌ AI-sounding vs ✅ human (teach the failure mode)

| ❌ AI register (what the framework used to produce) | ✅ Human |
|---|---|
| "Meet the 2026 CX-5 — AWD standard, premium interior, top safety ratings — from $310 bi-weekly." | "A brand-new CX-5 for $310 every two weeks, OAC. All-wheel drive comes standard, and so does the room for everyone." |
| "Drive home a new Mazda this summer and we'll get you on the green — pair it with 0% lease or 0.99% financing, plus a $500 rebate." | "Drive for show, putt for dough. Buy a new Mazda this summer and your 5-round golf pass is on us." |
| "Discover unbeatable savings and elevate your drive today." | "Good deal, good cars, right here in town. Come have a look." |

The tells to kill: "Meet the…/Discover/Elevate", em-dash cadence, feature triads, stacking every offer into one breath, gloss adjectives with no specifics.
