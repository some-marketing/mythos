# Guardrails

- Public-business research only; no private-person profiling.
- Do not search breached, credential, broker, or restricted datasets.
- Do not infer sensitive traits or identity from a single identifier.
- Every material claim requires a URL, retrieval date, source type, and confidence.
- Report observations and conflicts neutrally; absence of evidence is not evidence of absence.
- No outreach, account creation, scraping behind access controls, or external mutation.
- Redact personal phone numbers, emails, and addresses from shared framework artifacts unless strictly necessary and operator-authorized.
