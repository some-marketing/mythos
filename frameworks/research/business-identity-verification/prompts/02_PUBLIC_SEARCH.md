# Public business search

Mode: `FINDINGS_ONLY`

Search the business name, aliases, domain, and public business contact details across official websites, government registries, maps/business listings, trade directories, and archived public pages. Treat OSINT Framework links as leads, not proof. Record URL, access date, exact claim summary, source type, and whether the source is first- or third-party.

Do not use credential dumps, data brokers, breached data, targeted personal-social searches, or contact the subject. Do not infer identity from a phone number alone.

Output: an evidence log with unresolved collisions and confidence per finding.
