# Guardrails

- Public records and published business claims only.
- Do not investigate a private person beyond the public business role needed for the task.
- Do not contact, impersonate, surveil, or deanonymize the subject.
- Do not treat complaints, reviews, or a missing registry result as proof of wrongdoing.
- Verify licensing claims against the authoritative jurisdictional source where possible.
- Insurance is unverified unless supported by an authoritative public record or operator-supplied proof.
- Every material claim requires a URL, retrieval date, source type, and confidence.
- Redact personal identifiers from shared artifacts.
