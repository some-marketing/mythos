# Credential and reputation search

Mode: `FINDINGS_ONLY`

Check official licensing and registration portals, published trade memberships, business insurance representations, safety or permit records where publicly available, official business pages, and multiple review platforms. Capture the exact issuing authority, licence number only when public and relevant, status, dates, URL, and retrieval date. Treat insurance as `not publicly verified` unless an authoritative document or direct business-provided proof is available.

Do not scrape private data, bypass access controls, impersonate anyone, or contact the subject. A complaint or review is an allegation or opinion, not an established fact.

Output: an evidence log and conflict list.
