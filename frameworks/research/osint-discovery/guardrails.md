# Guardrails

- Public, lawful, purpose-bound discovery only.
- Every run must declare target, purpose, scope, and stop conditions.
- No doxxing, stalking, deanonymization, credential recovery, sensitive-trait inference, or private-person profiling.
- No breached data, credential dumps, data brokers, restricted sources, access-control bypass, or impersonation.
- No outreach or external mutation; this framework is findings-only/review-only.
- Every material observation needs a source URL, publisher, retrieval date, and confidence.
- Preserve contradictions and negative-search limitations.
- Redact personal identifiers from shared artifacts unless strictly necessary and authorized.
