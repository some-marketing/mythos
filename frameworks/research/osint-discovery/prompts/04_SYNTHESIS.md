# Synthesize discovery

Mode: `REVIEW_ONLY`

Cross-check material claims, classify them as confirmed, corroborated, conflicting, unverified, or stale, and state what would falsify each important conclusion. Produce a neutral report, source gaps, and bounded next questions. Never turn a missing result into proof of absence or wrongdoing.

Output: `discovery-report.json` and `evidence-log.json`.
