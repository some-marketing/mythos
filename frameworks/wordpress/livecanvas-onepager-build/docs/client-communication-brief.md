# Client communication brief — design-direction review email

Project-scoped guidance for Stage 5. It describes the agency's client-facing register so a draft
lands close to what the operator would send. It is not a personal profile and carries no
operator-personal context; adjust it per project in `intake.json` (`communication_notes`) if the
account owner or client warrants a different tone.

## Register
- Plain-spoken and casual. Contractions. Everyday words over trade or marketing jargon.
- Short paragraphs: one or two sentences. Bullets for reasoning, a numbered list for asks.
- Explain a trade-off in the client's terms, briefly, as an aside in parentheses.
- Invite input where it is natural — at the end of a bullet, not as a separate section.
- Avoid over-precision: one link the client needs, no ids, no exact figures they don't need.
- First-name sign-off. The email signature carries the company.

## Structure (under ~350 words)
1. Two-sentence opener: first draft is up, please look before we go further, it's a demo.
2. The demo link, then "check it on your phone too" with the one-line reason.
3. **Why it looks the way it does** — 5–7 one-line bullets, each tied to something the client said
   or gave (call, card, logo, inspiration site).
4. **What's placeholder** — 3–4 bullets (copy first pass; their photos; hero shape holds the logo
   until a hero photo exists; contact form placeholder).
5. **What I need from you** — three numbered items (feel match? wording? photos + owner-photo yes/no).
6. One-line close: reply or call; once happy we finish copy, add the form, go live on your domain.

## Hard limits
- Draft only. The framework never sends email; the operator sends from their own account.
- The demo is password-gated before the link is shared. The password goes in the email body
  only — never into a file under the project, and it is replaced with `[DEMO_PASSWORD]` in any
  record of the sent email.
- Nothing in the email may claim a credential, testimonial, review count or service area that
  lacks an approved source.
