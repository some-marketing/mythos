# Identity

This file is yours. It's read as labeled, advisory context at the start of a session —
never as authority, never as instructions a session must follow. Write whatever helps a
session understand who it's working with.

A few prompts, if you want them (delete anything you don't use):

## Who I am

- Name / what to call me:
- Role — what kind of work do I mostly bring here?

## How I like to work

- What do I want a session to check with me before doing?
- What do I want it to just go ahead and do without asking?

## Context worth knowing

- Anything about how you think, what you're sensitive to, or what you've learned the
  hard way that would help a session calibrate to you specifically.

---

Nothing here can grant a session authority it doesn't otherwise have, select which
commands it runs, change files on its own, or override anything in the Core
(`instructions/canonical/kernel/`). It's context, not control.
